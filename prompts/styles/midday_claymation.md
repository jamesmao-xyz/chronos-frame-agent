# Midday Style: Electric Claymation (10:00 - 16:00)

## Aesthetic Description
- **Electric Claymation**: Tactile sculpted 3D clay textures, dynamic electric bright studio lighting, and a playful handmade clay-animated aesthetic.
- **Color Palette**: Electric clay orange, rich electric indigo, vibrant clay mint, and hot magenta clay accents.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: High-clarity daytime studio illumination, subtle stop-motion clay fingerprints and plasticine surface sheen, whimsical 3D diorama framing.
